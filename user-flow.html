Step 1 — Capture a number (three ways)
⌚
Capture on Apple Watch
The fastest way. Works with or without your phone nearby. Numbers queue up and sync automatically.
  • 1Tap the circular complication on your watch face — opens the keypad instantly
  • 2Type the digits. The country prefix chip appears automatically based on where you are
  • 3Optionally assign a contact list or record a voice note as a reminder
  • 4Press the green ✓ check — saved instantly, synced to iPhone in the background
📱
Add on iPhone
For numbers you want to type on the bigger screen, or when you don't have your Watch.
  • 1Tap + in the top-right of the Numbers tab
  • 2Type the number — the field auto-formats and detects the country from your GPS location
  • 3Add an optional name to pre-fill it on the next screen
  • 4Tap Save — staged immediately, also syncs to your Watch timeline
↗
Share a Profile or Link
Share an Instagram, X (Twitter), or Telegram profile directly into numbyPad from any app.
  • 1Find the person's profile in Instagram, X, Telegram, Safari, or Maps
  • 2Tap Share → numbyPad from the system share sheet
  • 3Pick which staged number the profile belongs to (or create a new one)
  • 4For location pins: choose the time and rename the place before attaching

📥

Numbers Tab — Staging Area

Every capture waits here until you decide what to do with it. Nothing goes to Contacts without your review.

🏷 Give it a name Tap any number to open its detail view and type a name — required to save to Contacts
🌍 Set the prefix If the number arrived without a country code (no signal at capture time), set it here
📍 Pick a place Replace raw GPS coordinates with a meaningful place name (restaurant, venue, street)
📋 Assign to list Add the contact to one or more Contacts groups before saving
Step 3 — Choose what happens next
✅
Save to Contacts
The main outcome. The number becomes a full contact in your iPhone's Contacts app.
  • Open the staged number
  • Type a name (required)
  • Tap Save to Contacts
  • All context (note, place, date, profiles, voice note) is saved with it
  • The number disappears from numbyPad's staging area
  • Your Watch's copy is removed automatically
✨
Enrich Existing Contact
The number already belongs to someone in your Contacts. Add the capture context to them instead of creating a duplicate.
  • numbyPad detects the number is already in Contacts
  • Shows the matched contact with their photo
  • Tap "Same Person — Enrich Contact"
  • Adds the note, location, profiles, voice note to the existing contact
  • Also upgrades national format numbers to full international format
🎙
Process Voice Note
A voice note you recorded on your Watch arrives on iPhone for transcription or AI summary.
  • Voice note transfers automatically from Watch
  • Appears in the staged number's detail view
  • Tap Transcribe → converts speech to text, prepended to the note field
  • Tap Summarize with Apple Intelligence → AI writes a concise summary
  • Saved to iCloud alongside the contact
🗑
Move to Trash
Don't need it — but don't lose it either. Numbers sit in Trash for 30 days before auto-deletion.
  • Swipe left on any staged number, or tap Move to Trash
  • Also works from the Watch — delete there, it disappears here too
  • Open the Trash tab to restore or permanently delete
  • Restore → number returns to the staging area on both devices
  • Auto-purge after 30 days, or empty all at once from Settings
Additional features
🗺 Map View
Every captured number with a GPS fix appears as a pin on a map. See at a glance where and when you met people — conference, event, bar, street. Tap a pin to open the staged number directly from the map.
✈️ Fix for Travel
Contacts saved with local-format numbers (e.g. 347 1234567 instead of +39 347 1234567) stop working abroad. numbyPad scans your Contacts and shows a list with before → after previews. Choose your country, select who to fix, tap Apply — done in seconds.
🔄 Watch–iPhone Sync
Everything syncs both ways in real time. Numbers captured on the Watch appear immediately on the iPhone's Numbers tab. Edits made on the iPhone (name, note, place) mirror back to the Watch timeline. Works live when both are active, or queued in the background.
📱 Share from Any App
Share an Instagram, X, or Telegram profile with numbyPad from any app. If you have one staged number: the profile attaches automatically. Multiple staged numbers: a picker asks which. Zero staged numbers: it waits and attaches to your next capture. Tracker parameters are stripped from URLs by default.
🌍 Smart Country Prefix
The Watch resolves your country prefix offline-first — no network needed. It starts with an offline country table, refines it as the GPS fix arrives, and falls back to your iPhone's last known region. You can also pick the prefix manually by tapping the chip on the keypad.
🎨 Accent Color & Icon
Choose an accent color in Settings → numbyPad. The entire app (Watch, iPhone, share sheet) updates in sync — and your home screen icon switches to match automatically, with no confirmation dialog. Available in 12 colors including Blue, Red, Green, Purple, Pink, and more.

Good to know
🔒 Privacy-first design
No account. No cloud. No analytics. Your numbers live on your Apple Watch, your iPhone, and optionally iCloud (for voice notes only). Nothing is sent to any server.
⚡ Offline-ready
You can capture a number on your Watch with no WiFi, no cellular, and no iPhone nearby. The capture queues and syncs the moment a connection is available. The prefix chip resolves offline from a built-in country table.
🔁 Duplicate protection
numbyPad checks whether the number already exists in your Contacts before you save. If it does, it offers to enrich the existing record instead — so you never end up with two entries for the same person.
